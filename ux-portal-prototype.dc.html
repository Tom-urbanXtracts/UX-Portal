NOT A DECIDED CAPABILITY
The mechanism is designed and the button is here so it can be reviewed, but it does not function and should not until three people have answered one question. A rep approving your order means the seller is approving the buyer’s purchase commitment — a segregation-of-duties and contracting question before it is a feature: who is committed, on what authority, and what your written authorisation actually says.

Open with sales leadership, the CCO and Legal. It also needs a standing written authorisation held on your account — a document under Documents, not a click on this screen.

What a delegate can never do

A delegate approves an order. A delegate does not lift a block, and no delegation can be configured to.

License gate
A location whose licence has lapsed cannot be ordered for, whoever holds approval. Northgate is the live example.
Account ordering hold
An explicit portal store status may block submission at order entry. A QuickBooks balance is visible context and never changes that hold automatically.
Available quantity
An order above what exists is not approvable into existence.

The clock

How long an approval may sit
Not set. How long, and where it escalates, is an open decision with sales leadership. Until it is answered an order can wait indefinitely, which is the failure the clock exists to prevent.
What an order will show
Whoever holds authority and until when, named on the order itself and kept there permanently. An order approved under a delegation says so on its face for the rest of its life.
When authority is withdrawn
It takes effect at once. An approval already in flight still names the person who held it — the record does not rewrite itself.

A read writes the same audit event an export writes — scope, row count and the field list. You can read your own entries under Your records, which is also where a revocation lands.
Scoped to your licences
A credential reaches the licences it was issued for and no others. Narrowing a credential to one location is the normal case, not the exception.
Rate limit and page size
Not yet set. The numbers are carried as an open item under administration, alongside the unresolved question of whether a chain holding several licences resolves to one credential.
Revocation is immediate
Revoking stops reads at once rather than at the end of a session, and the credential cannot be un-revoked \u2014 issue a new one.

How financial visibility affects ordering

Separate controls
A non-zero or past-due QuickBooks balance is displayed here but never creates an order hold by itself. The portal’s explicit store ordering status remains the only receivables-related gate.
Buyer scope
Buyers see invoices and payments only for stores assigned to their profile. A store without a direct QuickBooks customer mapping stays blank instead of falling back to an organisation-wide customer and leaking another store.
Payments
This release displays accounting history only. It does not collect a payment, store a bank account, or create a QuickBooks transaction.
Source
Every live figure comes from the last fully successful server-side QuickBooks snapshot. A failed refresh leaves the prior complete snapshot visible with a stale warning.
HOW TO PAY IS NOT ON THIS SCREEN
Banking, remittance and signatory details live in the onboarding record and are not rendered anywhere in the portal, here included. Settling an invoice goes through the contact on your account, and this screen tells you what is owed rather than how to send money.

WHAT IS NEVER COUNTED
Nothing ties a budtender lookup to a consumer. The kiosk has no person behind it and gains none here: a lookup is counted per device and per day, and the thing looked up is never joined to who was standing there. There is no identifier for a shopper anywhere in this, and adding one later would be a decision with its own sign-off rather than a schema change.
WHAT THIS PROTOTYPE CANNOT SEE
One session is one observation. A median and a ninetieth percentile need a population, and the interesting half of the gate question — what a blocked user did in the next ten minutes — needs a real deployment and a real telephone log. What is proven here is that the events exist and can be counted at the moment they happen, which is the part that cannot be added afterwards.
