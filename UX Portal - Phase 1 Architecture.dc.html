The decisions, in one page

One wall
Any internal user sees cost. No external view renders it — nor margin percent, discount depth, floor-price warnings, rate cards or ownership code. There is no internal tiering.
Not rendered
A restricted route, nav entry or field does not exist for that role. Nothing appears greyed out, and the interface is never the enforcement mechanism.
Gates decide first
License, release and AR gates determine availability before any screen renders. Every block states a reason and a path forward.
Released means two things
Orderable requires package status available and lab result TestPassed. Both. Nothing else makes a product appear in a catalog.
Tag is the lot key
The compliance tag identifies the lot externally. Batch number and lot ID are enrichment shown when present — they are absent on most inventory today.
Four states out, ten in
Stores see Placed, Approved, Shipped, Received. Operations keeps the full Canix set so the portal and Canix never disagree.
01

Primary user goals

Each goal below is one sentence a user would say out loud, paired with the shortest path the portal must offer. The hero path is the first one: catalog to product detail to lot and COA to order. Everything else is arranged so it does not obstruct that path.

Buyer
“I need to restock the vape carts that sell, without calling anyone.”
Catalog → quick-add → draft → submit. Two screens, no rep.
Store owner
“What did we spend, across all four shops, and what needs my approval?”
Dashboard with spend, per-location split, approval queue.
Budtender
“A customer is holding this jar and asking what’s in it.”
Scan the tag → potency, terpenes, COA. No login, no price.
Account rep
“Which of my accounts has stopped ordering, and what did they drop?”
Account view with order interval, product mix, visit notes.
Quality
“Nothing unreleased or failed can be sitting in a store’s catalog.”
Release queue, quarantine reasons, COA versions.
Finance
“What does this product actually make us, by account?”
Cost and margin by cost object, order and account.

NOTE
Server-side authorization is required and is the actual control. Field-level filtering happens before a response leaves the server — including in search results, exports, notification payloads and error messages. Interface hiding is a usability measure, never the security mechanism. When an administrator views another user’s screen, cost is withheld even though that administrator could see it in their own view, so an impersonated screenshot can never carry cost out of the building.
06

Gates

Six gates. Each states where it applies and what the blocked user sees, because a block without an explanation is the single largest generator of workarounds — the user calls a rep, and the portal has lost.

License expired at a location
Blocks ordering for that location only; other locations continue
“Ordering for this shop is paused — its license record expired on 12 Aug. Upload the renewal, or contact your account manager.” Catalog stays browsable.
Not released
Availability — the SKU is not rendered at all
Nothing. An unreleased or failed lot has no external presence, so there is no state to explain. Internally it appears in the release queue with its quarantine reason.
AR past due
Fires at order entry, not at shipping
“This order can’t be submitted while the account has a past-due balance.” The buyer sees that the gate fired and who to contact; the balance itself is shown only to the owner.
Requires owner approval
On submit: buyer-submitted, over threshold, over available quantity, or AR past due
“Sent to [owner] for approval.” The draft stays visible and editable, the trigger is named, and the owner is notified.
COA pending or amended
On the lot panel and the public resolver
Pending: “Testing in progress for this lot — results aren’t published yet.” No promise of a date, no interpretation. Amended: the current version is shown with an amendment marker, and accounts that bought the lot are notified.
Inventory changed after adding
On draft review and again on submit
“Available quantity for this line dropped to 18 since you added it.” The line is flagged, not silently reduced, and the buyer chooses.

Kiosk mode

A shared floor tablet has no individual user, which means it cannot carry attribution. Rather than pretend otherwise, kiosk mode is education-only: nothing it can do requires an audit trail. The device is authorised to a location by an urbanXtracts administrator, and revoking it is a single internal action rather than a request to the store.

If a location’s license lapses, its kiosk keeps education and COA access — it never had ordering to lose, and cutting off product information would push staff back to unverified sources. Typed tag entry requires the full tag: 80 of your active tags share their last six digits, so a partial match resolves to a list, never to a guess.

Open items

Each item names the question, its class, whether it stops a gate, and which gate. Owners are proposed. Nothing here is treated as decided.

BUSINESS DECISIONBLOCKING
Order-value threshold above which owner approval fires. Proposed owner: sales leadership. Blocks: the order-submission gate — without a number, every order either needs approval or none does.
BUSINESS DECISIONBLOCKING
Definition of past-due for the AR gate — days, amount, or both. Proposed owner: finance. Blocks: the AR gate at order entry.
INFORMATION REQUIREDBLOCKING
Where parsed potency and terpene values will live, and who maintains them. Proposed owner: quality. Blocks: the potency and profile layer of the product detail page — the largest section of the hero screen currently has no source.
TECHNICAL VALIDATION REQUIREDBLOCKING
The per-account rate card does not exist as a record today, so catalog price is derived from the most recent qualifying order line — excluding samples, cancellations, returns and lines where discount exceeds price — with the derivation date shown. Proposed owner: sales operations. Blocks: showing a price at all; a derived price is a stopgap, not a pricing policy.
FINANCE DECISIONBLOCKING
Whether a margin figure may be shown when its cost components are incomplete, and at what coverage. Proposed owner: finance. Blocks: the internal economics view. Labor cost is effectively unpopulated, so a margin computed today would be wrong rather than partial — the Phase 3 view carries clearly-labelled synthetic figures until this is answered.
CCO SIGN-OFF REQUIREDBLOCKING
What the public COA page displays, how long a printed code keeps resolving, and whether retest history is shown. Owner: CCO. Blocks: publication of the public resolver. The mechanism is designed for either answer; nothing publishes while this is open.
CANIX VALIDATION REQUIRED
Whether case quantity, minimum order and lead time are readable per item, and where. Proposed owner: operations. Affects: order validation — without it the portal cannot enforce case increments.
BUSINESS DECISION
Reorder interval basis: the account’s own average against days since last order, with the basis stated on screen. No sell-through data exists, so no velocity claim is made. Proposed owner: sales leadership.
TECHNICAL VALIDATION REQUIRED
Whether a store organisation can be resolved from license numbers alone when a chain holds several licenses. Proposed owner: administration. Affects: location switching and org-level AR.
BUSINESS DECISION
Multi-factor authentication was left open; this document assumes none in Phase 1 and no personal login at all on kiosk devices. Proposed owner: administration. Recorded as an assumption, not a decision.
INFORMATION REQUIRED
Which lot and batch identifiers will exist going forward, given that most active packages carry neither today. The design treats the tag as sufficient identity so the portal works either way. Proposed owner: operations.

Acceptance

Criteria
Every visibility derives from a stated rule. Every gate has a user-visible reason and a path forward. Every screen element names its source. No view can render another organisation’s data by any route, including search, export, notification payloads and error messages.
Definition of done
Phase 1 closes when the matrix, gates and flows are approved and every blocking open item is either answered or explicitly accepted as open into Phase 2. Approval of this document is not approval to build the economics view.
Required tests
An expired license blocks ordering at that location only. Unreleased product is not visible. The AR gate fires at order entry, not at shipping. One store cannot see another’s orders. A blocked user is given a path forward. Cost is absent from every external view and from every impersonated view.
Evidence & owner
Evidence: this document, plus the connector reads dated 2026-08-24 behind each verified field. Owner: whoever holds portal delivery. Approver: to be named — recorded as proposed.
Every control described here is designed. None is live, and none should be described as compliant or sufficient.
