The real constraint
Almost nothing left is design work. Three data sources and six decisions gate everything that would make this production-grade.
What is already true
Release rules, order lifecycle, segregation, gates, audit and the validation queue all work against measured structure.
Definition of amazing
A buyer reorders in under thirty seconds without calling a rep, and a budtender answers a customer from the jar in their hand.
Wave 1

Unblock the hero flow

Nothing else matters as much as this: catalog to product to lot to order, with a price a buyer can trust. Three items, none of them mine.

Build the per-account rate card
A record of SKU by account with price, effective date and an approver. Today price is derived from the last qualifying order line, which is a stopgap that quotes nothing for a new account or a new SKU. This single record also retires the price-on-request path, the price-changed warning and half the pricing caveats in the interface.

From you

Three things only you can supply

Product photography
Every card and product page is an arch-framed placeholder. Photography is what makes a wholesale catalog feel like a premium one.
Training and FAQ copy
Process education is written. Product-specific talking points need your words — nothing about effects or benefits should be generated.
Brand type and any secondary palette
Archivo is the design system’s face, not confirmed as yours. If the brand has its own typeface or an approved secondary color, it belongs here before the developer handoff.
The honest summary
The portal already proves its architecture: role separation holds, gates block with reasons, orders move through their whole lifecycle, and the interface refuses to invent a number it does not have. What stands between this and amazing is not more screens — it is a rate card, a potency record, a sell-through feed, and six decisions. Wave 3 makes it feel finished; waves 1 and 2 make it true.
