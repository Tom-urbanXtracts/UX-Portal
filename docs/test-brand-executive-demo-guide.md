# Test Brand executive demonstration

## Before the meeting

1. Open `https://portal.urbanxtracts.com` in a private browser window.
2. Keep a second private window available for the Internal Administrator portion.
3. Use the isolated demonstration accounts:
   - Brand: `Marketing@urbanxtract.com`
   - Internal: `Internal@urbanxtracts.com`
   - Password for both: `UXDemo43!`
4. Confirm the selected Brand is **Test Brand** before creating or changing any demonstration record.

## Part 1 — Brand experience

### 1. Home

1. Sign in as `Marketing@urbanxtract.com`.
2. Start on **Home**.
3. Explain that this page is the Brand's summary of its own activity only.
4. Point out the onboarding state, active products, open purchase orders, inventory position, sales trend, and items needing attention.

### 2. Company & Onboarding

1. Open **Company & Onboarding**.
2. Show the six sections: Company Profile; Contacts & Authorized Users; Billing & Remittance; Qualification Information; License & Operating Information; and Document Upload.
3. Explain that a Brand can save partial progress and submit each section separately.
4. Point out that the assigned urbanXtracts Brand member is displayed but cannot be changed by the Brand.
5. Explain that a returned section becomes editable and must be resubmitted.

### 3. Agreements

1. Open **Agreements**.
2. Expand two agreement requirements.
3. Show the current document first, followed by older versions.
4. Open an individual document and point out its upload date and version.
5. Explain that urbanXtracts controls approval and that previous versions remain available for audit history.

### 4. Products, vendors, and controlled documents

1. Open **Products & SKUs** and show that the Brand sees finished products intended for sale, not internal bulk or plant-material inventory.
2. Open **Vendors & Components** and explain how component manufacturers and supporting qualification records are organized.
3. Open **Marketing Materials** and show Brand assets such as logos and approved sales content.
4. Open **Quality & Operations Documents** and demonstrate individual download and the combined ZIP download.

### 5. Catalog and purchase order

1. Open **Catalog and orders**.
2. Search or filter the Test Brand catalog.
3. Open a product card and point out the product image, product name, units per case, price, and order quantity.
4. Add more than one item to the cart.
5. Change a quantity and confirm the line and order totals update.
6. Save the order as a draft, leave the page, return, and reopen the draft.
7. Submit the purchase order.
8. Open the submitted order and show its status history.
9. Use **Report an issue** to demonstrate how a Brand can raise an order-specific question without editing the submitted order.

### 6. Performance and finance

1. Open **Inventory** and explain that only Brand-scoped finished-goods inventory is visible.
2. Open **Sales & Distribution** and demonstrate the available filters and milestones. Explain that retailer and store identities remain confidential.
3. Open **Financials** and show the read-only QuickBooks-backed invoices, credits, balances, aging, and payment history.
4. Explain that the portal displays financial records but does not let the Brand edit QuickBooks data.

### 7. Requests and support

1. Open **Tasks & Requests** and show assigned work, due dates, and status.
2. Open **Support & Issues** and submit or inspect a portal issue.
3. Explain the difference: order issues stay connected to an order; portal issues cover access, data, or product behavior.

## Part 2 — Internal controls

### 8. Open Test Brand management

1. Sign out of the Brand account.
2. Sign in as `Internal@urbanxtracts.com`.
3. Open **Brand administration**.
4. Select **Test Brand** and choose **Manage Brand workspace**.
5. Explain that this is an audited management session, not the read-only **View as Brand** mode.

### 9. Review Brand onboarding

1. Open **Company & Onboarding**.
2. Select each submitted section.
3. Demonstrate **Approve** on a complete section and **Return for changes** on another.
4. Show that the Internal team controls the assigned Brand member.
5. Explain that the Brand profile is approved only when all required sections are approved.

### 10. Process the purchase order

1. End the Brand management session.
2. Open **Brand purchase orders** from the Internal sidebar.
3. Select the purchase order submitted during the Brand portion.
4. Review the Brand, line items, quantities, requested dates, and notes.
5. Approve the order and advance it through the available operating milestones.
6. Explain that every transition is authorized and recorded, and that the portal owns the request and line history.

### 11. Show work queues and evidence

1. Open **Work queues** to show Brand requests, order issues, and portal issues that need action.
2. Open **Users and access** to explain how Internal and Brand access are separated.
3. Open **Audit history** to show the recorded management and workflow actions.
4. Open **Release readiness**.
5. Show the release decision, readiness percentage, P0 blockers, P1 work, controlled holds, and completed evidence.
6. Expand **Not Started**, **In Progress**, and **Completed** to demonstrate owners, due dates, evidence, comments, and live automated checks.

## Closing message for executives

The demonstration should leave three points clear:

1. A Brand can onboard, maintain documents, review its finished products and performance, create a multi-line purchase order, and track its own financial and distribution information in one workspace.
2. urbanXtracts controls approvals, assignments, order processing, access, evidence, and exceptions without exposing another Brand's information.
3. Canix and QuickBooks remain authoritative for their operational and accounting data, while the portal becomes the controlled interface used by Brands and the Internal team.
