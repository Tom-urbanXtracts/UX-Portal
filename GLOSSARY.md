# UX OS

UX OS connects Internal staff, Brand partners, and licensed retailers through scoped workspaces.

## Language

**Retailer organization**:
The retail business whose approved users may operate one or more licensed store locations.
_Avoid_: Store when referring to the whole business, user account

**Store location**:
One licensed retail location within a retailer organization, with its own qualification, delivery, ordering, and user-assignment scope.
_Avoid_: Retailer organization when referring to a single location

**Sellable-item quantity**:
The current quantity of a published product approved for ordering within an authorized Store location, distinct from package-level or internal inventory detail.
_Avoid_: Exact inventory, package quantity

**Workspace membership**:
A person's approved relationship to one organization in Internal, Brand, or Store, including their role and access status.
_Avoid_: Workspace selection as a synonym for permission, global role

**Accounting customer**:
A QuickBooks customer identity used to associate accounting records with an approved portal organization or store location.
_Avoid_: Portal user, retailer organization as an interchangeable identity

**Store order**:
A retailer's product request for one licensed delivery location, subject to its commercial, approval, inventory, and compliance gates.
_Avoid_: Brand purchase order, invoice, payment

**Brand purchase order**:
A Brand organization's request to urbanXtracts under its approved product and commercial workflow.
_Avoid_: Store order, automatic accounting transaction
